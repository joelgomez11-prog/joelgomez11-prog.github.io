Mav privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Who uses Mav

Mav is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner. It is not available to anyone else, and no other user's data is collected.

What Mav accesses

  • Gmail, with the gmail.readonly permission: to find bills, due dates and messages its owner asks about.
  • Google Calendar, with the calendar.readonly permission: to tell its owner what is on his schedule.

Mav cannot send, delete or modify email, and cannot create or change calendar events.

Where the data goes

The data is stored and processed on its owner's own computer at home, using AI models that run on that computer. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Optional features that would send a question to an outside service are off by default and can only be turned on by the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mav's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Mav's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete its stored data from his computer.
